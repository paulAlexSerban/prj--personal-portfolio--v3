## Answer:

Model answer:

Implementations rot and get rewritten, but the reasoning behind a decision remains useful long after the surrounding code has changed. An ADR captures status, context, drivers, options considered, the decision, and consequences so future maintainers can revisit or supersede the choice with the original trade-offs in view.

## Explanation:

Strong answers emphasize preserving _why_ over _how_, and mention revisability (Proposed -> Accepted -> Deprecated/Superseded).
