## Overview

- Architecture = structural decisions that constrain future change
- Evaluate with cohesion (high) and coupling (low)
- Document significant choices as ADRs

## Key Concepts

### Components, Connectors, Configurations

- **Component**: unit of computation with an interface
- **Connector**: communication mechanism (HTTP, queue, call)
- **Configuration**: assembly of the above into a system

### Common Patterns

- Layered / n-tier - good for small teams on one deployable
- Microservices - extract only when scaling or ownership needs diverge
- Event-driven - prefer publish over synchronous call chains when temporal decoupling matters

## Trade-offs

- Complexity has a cost; YAGNI applies to architecture too
- Distributed failures are partial and probabilistic - plan for retries and idempotency
